About 901 Bankruptcy

Our Approach

Bankruptcy Guidance for the Mid-South

901 Bankruptcy works with individuals and business owners across Memphis and the Mid-South who need a clear, honest path through debt. We take the time to understand your full financial picture before recommending a chapter or a strategy — because the right answer depends on your goals, not a template.

We handle both consumer cases (Chapter 7 and Chapter 13) and business reorganizations (Chapter 11), so whether you're an individual behind on bills or a business owner trying to keep the doors open, you're working with someone who speaks that language.
